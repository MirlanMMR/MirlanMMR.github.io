Home

“Home” shows how much money is on hand, how much went out and came in over the period, and on what. New records are added from here too.

What is on the screen, top to bottom

  • The date or period: day, week, month, quarter or year. The length of the period is chosen in the side menu.
  • “On hand”: the balance at the end of the period, that is the opening balance plus all income minus all expenses.
  • The period's totals: two figures in one frame, expenses with “−” and income with “+”.
  • The chart: a circle split into categories.
  • A row of category icons under the chart.
  • The “Expense” and “Income” buttons.

How to change the period

Swipe left or right across the top of the screen or across the middle of the chart. Left is the next period, right is the previous one. You cannot move into the future.

How to read the chart

  • Each slice is one category; its size is the category's share of the period's spending.
  • Tap the income figure in the totals box and the chart shows income. Tap the expenses and it goes back.
  • The row under the chart holds only the categories that have records in this period, largest first.
  • Scroll the row or move a finger along the ring and the chart turns. The slice of the category that lands in the middle of the row slides out, and its amount and share appear in the center of the circle.
  • Tap a slice or an icon and that category moves to the middle.
  • Press and hold a slice and the center shows its share.
  • The grey slice is records without a category.

The first slot in the row is empty: when it is in the middle, the center of the circle shows the period's total.

How to open a category's records

Bring the category to the middle and tap the center of the circle. If there is one record, it opens for editing. If there are several, a list appears: tap the one you need.

How to add a record

  1. Tap “Expense” or “Income”.
  2. Enter the amount. You can calculate in the field: 990+180. The “=” key puts in the result and saves the calculation to “Calculation history”.
  3. Pick a category or create a new one right there: “New”.
  4. If you like, add a subcategory, a note, a wallet, or change the date.
  5. Tap “Save”.

If you close the card without saving, what you typed is kept: next time the app offers to pick up where you left off. “Start over” clears the card.

A record can be saved without a category; it goes to the grey “No category” place.

A quick way: press and hold the app icon on the phone's home screen and choose “Add expense” or “Add income”.

The color of the “On hand” amount

ColorWhat it means
Normalthe money is above the lower threshold, or no threshold is set
Yellow, with “!”the balance is close to the threshold
Redthe balance is below the threshold or below zero

The threshold is set in the currency card: “Settings” → “Currencies” → “Lower balance threshold”. More in “Wallets and currencies”.

The “Attention” line

It appears when something needs action: a payment is due, a receipt is expected, a category's limit is used up, the balance is below the threshold. Example: “Attention: payments: 2, limits: 1”. The line can be turned off in “Settings”, section “Reminders”.

Search and the side menu

The button with three bars at the top left opens the side menu: period, currency converter, currency, wallet. The magnifier opens search by note, category, amount and date.
