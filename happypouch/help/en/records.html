Records

“Records” is the list of everything entered for the period, grouped by category. This is where records are found, corrected and deleted.

What is on the screen

  • The period at the top, the same one as on “Home” and in “Statistics”. Move it with the arrows or by swiping left and right.
  • “On hand”: the balance at the end of the period.
  • Wallets, if you have any: the balance of each and the transfer button.
  • The “All / Expenses / Income” switch: what the list shows.
  • Categories with their amount for the period, largest first.
  • Transfers between wallets, at the bottom of the list, only in “All”.

How to reach a record

Tap a category.

  • If it has one record, the record opens at once.
  • If the period is a day, the list of records opens.
  • If the period is longer, the category opens in parts: a year into quarters, a quarter into months, a month into weeks, a week into days. Keep tapping until you reach the records.

How to edit a record

Tap it. In the card you can change the amount, category, subcategory, date, wallet and note, then “Save”.

An expense cannot be turned into income or the other way round. If a record was entered as the wrong kind, delete it and enter it again.

How to delete a record

Open the record and tap “Delete”. The record goes to the trash.

  • Right after deleting, “Undo delete” shows at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds.
  • The record stays in the trash for 90 days: “Settings” → “Trash”. There you can restore it or delete it forever.
  • The “Delete this record?” question is turned off in the same place: “Ask before deleting”.

How to add a record from here

The round “+” button at the bottom right. In the card choose “Expense” or “Income”. The button hides while you scroll the list down and comes back when you scroll up.

Search

The magnifier at the top. You can search by note, category, wallet, amount and date. The result narrows by kind (expense or income), category and time: month, 3 months, year, all time. Under the search line is the number of records found and their total.

Icons only

“Settings” → “Icons only” replaces category names with icons on “Home” and in “Records”, so that someone glancing at the screen cannot tell what the money goes on.
