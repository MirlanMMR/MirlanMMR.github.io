Happy Pouch Help · Security

Security and privacy

Records are stored only on your phone. The app has no account, no server and no sign-up; tracking needs no internet.

Where the data is

  • In the app on the phone. It is sent nowhere.
  • In the backups on the phone: the “Documents/HappyPouch” folder. If you signed in to Google they are encrypted (see below); without signing in they are plain.
  • On your Google Drive, only if you turned on auto-backup yourself.

More about backups in “Backups and a new phone”.

Backup encryption

When you sign in to Google (optional, “Settings” → “Auto-backup to Google Drive”), the app creates a random key and puts it on your Google Drive, in a hidden folder of the app. Drive's interface does not show it and other apps cannot read it. For this Google asks for the permission “See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive” when you sign in. Every backup is encrypted with that key (AES-256): on the phone, on Drive and with “Save somewhere else…”.

  • A backup file that ends up in the wrong hands, through a messenger, a cloud or another app, cannot be opened without your Google account.
  • On a new phone sign in to the same Google account: the app fetches the key itself and opens the backups. There is no separate password, and a forgotten Google password is recovered through Google.
  • The first time on a new phone the internet is needed to fetch the key.
  • Without signing in to Google there is no encryption: backups are plain, as before.
  • Backups made before encryption was on stay plain until newer ones push them out.

Do not delete the app's data in Google Drive's settings (“Manage apps” → Happy Pouch → “Delete hidden app data”), and do not give up the Google account for good: without the key encrypted backups cannot be opened. A copy of the key is also kept on the phone, but it is lost on a reinstall or a new phone.

Login protection

“Settings” → “Login protection”. The app then unlocks the same way as your phone: PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face.

  • The app has no password of its own: it uses the phone's lock. If that is off, the app asks you to enable screen lock in the phone's settings first.
  • “Ask again if the app was closed” sets how long after leaving the app the check is needed again: immediately, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes.
  • If you cancel the check, the app stays locked; the “Open” button asks again.

Login protection locks the app's screen. The backup files are locked by encryption, which works if you signed in to Google.

Icons only

“Settings” → “Icons only”. On “Home” and in “Records” icons stay in place of category names. Someone glancing at the screen sees amounts, but not what they are for.

What Google can reach

Auto-backup to Google Drive is optional. If you turn it on:

  • on Drive the app sees only the files it created itself; other files are closed to it;
  • backups and the key sit on your Drive, and the developer has no access to them; backups are encrypted with that key;
  • “Sign out” stops automatic backups; saved backups stay on Drive, and you can delete them in Google Drive itself.

Reminders

A regular payment notification is visible on the phone's lock screen. If that is unwanted, choose “In the app only” or the badge dot in “Settings”, section “Reminders”.

Deleting

  • Deleted records and categories stay in the trash for 90 days: “Settings” → “Trash”. “Clear” erases them forever.
  • “Settings” → “Data” → “Delete all data” erases everything on the phone. Backups on Drive and in the “Documents/HappyPouch” folder stay; delete them separately if needed.

Sending a file through another service

A backup file sent to a messenger, a cloud or an AI assistant is encrypted if you signed in to Google: it cannot be read without your account. Without signing in the file is plain, and that service sees it. A table exported to CSV is not protected by the encryption.
