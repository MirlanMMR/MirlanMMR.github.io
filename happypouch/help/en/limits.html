Limits

A limit is the amount you are ready to spend on a category in a month. The app forbids nothing: it shows how much is left and warns when the limit is near.

How to set a limit

“Settings” → “Expense categories” → the category → “Monthly limit”. Enter the amount and tap “Save”. An empty field means “No limit”.

  • The limit runs over a calendar month, from the first day.
  • Subcategories count toward the category.
  • Each currency has its own limit field: “Limit, USD”, “Limit, EUR”. A limit applies to records in its currency.

Where to see how much is left

In “Statistics”, the “Limits” block for the current month. For each category with a limit there is a bar and a line: “spent of limit · left” or “exceeded by …”. Categories are listed from the most used up.

When the app warns

SpentWhat happens
80% of the limitA warning: “Category: 80% of limit”.
100% and more“Limit exceeded: category”.

The warning shows:

  • in “Records”, as a strip above the list;
  • on “Home”, in the “Attention” line: “limits: 1”;
  • as a notification, if “Regular notifications” or the badge dot is chosen in “Settings”, section “Reminders”.

How to change or remove it

In the same place, the category card: correct the amount or erase it. Records do not change.

A limit is not the lower balance threshold. A limit is about one category's spending in a month; the threshold is about all the money on hand. The threshold is covered in “Wallets and currencies”.
