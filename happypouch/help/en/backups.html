Happy Pouch Help · Backups and a new phone

Backups and a new phone

All records are stored only on your phone. So that a broken phone, a reinstall or a new phone does not lose them, the app makes backups in two places, independent of each other.

Backups on this phone

Always on; nothing to set up. The app saves a backup by itself to the “Documents/HappyPouch” folder.

  • They do not depend on Google or the internet.
  • You can see them in the “Files” app.
  • They stay on the phone even if Happy Pouch is uninstalled.

In “Settings”, section “Backups on this phone”, there are three buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
Back up nowSaves a backup at once, without waiting for the next one.
RestoreShows the list of backups; the one you pick replaces all current data. “Choose file…” is there too, for a backup kept somewhere else.
Save somewhere else…Saves a backup wherever you choose: a flash drive, a messenger, another cloud.

Auto-backup to Google Drive

Optional. Turn it on in “Settings”, section “Auto-backup to Google Drive”: the “Back up automatically” switch asks you to sign in to Google.

  • A backup is saved when you close the app, and once a day if there is something new.
  • It sits on your Google Drive, in a folder named like “happy-pouch-backup-…”. The key every backup is encrypted with is kept apart, in the app's hidden folder on your Drive; more in “Security and privacy”.
  • On Drive the app sees only the files it created itself. It cannot access anything else.
  • “Back up now” makes a backup at once; “Restore” shows the list of backups on Drive.
  • “Sign out” stops automatic backups. Backups already saved stay on Drive, and the data on the phone does not change.

If the app says “Backup is out of date”, your latest records are not in it. Tap “Back up now”, especially before a reset or switching phones.

Moving to a new phone

Through Google Drive. On the old phone tap “Back up now”. On the new one install Happy Pouch, turn on auto-backup with the same Google account and tap “Restore”. The app fetches the key itself.

With a file, without Google. On the old phone tap “Save somewhere else…” and send the file to yourself in any way you like. On the new one open “Backups on this phone” → “Restore” → “Choose file…”. If the backups were encrypted, first turn on auto-backup with the same Google account; otherwise the app says the backup is encrypted. A backup file can also be opened straight from the “Files” app or from a messenger: Happy Pouch offers to load it.

What is in a backup

Records, categories and their kinds, wallets, transfers, currencies and converter rates, regular payments, limits, the trash, the “My region” figures.

Restoring replaces everything in the app now; it does not add to it. The app asks you to confirm.

A CSV export is not a backup

“Settings” → “Data” → “Export to CSV (Excel)” saves a table of records to open in Excel. The app cannot be fully restored from it: the table has no wallets, payments or settings.

Delete all data

“Settings” → “Data” → “Delete all data” erases records, categories, currencies and payments on the phone. Backups on Drive and in the “Documents/HappyPouch” folder stay.
