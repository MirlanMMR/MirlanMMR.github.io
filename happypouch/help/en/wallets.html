Wallets and currencies

A currency is what you count money in. A wallet is where it sits: cash, a card, a deposit. Wallets are optional: you can simply track everything in one currency.

Currencies

“Settings” → “Currencies”. On first launch the app creates your country's currency.

  • Add: the “+” button in the section header. A name or a code is enough: “USD”, “euro”.
  • Home: the currency you are looking at right now. Mark it in the list; “Home”, “Records” and “Statistics” show the records in it.
  • Opening balance: the amount tracking starts from. After that the balance is counted for you.
  • Lower balance threshold: an optional limit line. When your money gets close to it, the “On hand” amount turns yellow; below it, red.

Deleting a currency deletes all records, payments and wallets in it. The last currency cannot be deleted.

Wallets

“Settings” → “Wallets” → “+”. A wallet has a name, a currency, a current balance and an icon.

  • There can be as many wallets as you like: two cards, cash, a deposit.
  • Each wallet has its own currency. Cash in euros and cash in dollars are two wallets.
  • You can track crypto the same way: add a currency such as USDT, and a wallet in it. Amounts are stored with two decimal places, so small fractions of a bitcoin cannot be recorded.

How to assign a record to a wallet

In the record card, buttons for the wallets of that currency appear under the amount. Tap the one you need. If you pick none, the record has no wallet: it counts toward the currency's total balance, but not toward any wallet's balance.

How to look at one wallet

Open the side menu and pick the wallet in the “Wallet” section. “Home”, “Records” and “Statistics” show only that wallet, and “On hand” shows its balance. “All wallets” brings back the overall view.

Transfers

A transfer moves money from one wallet to another and counts as neither an expense nor income.

  • To make a transfer, use the two-arrow icon next to the “On hand” amount on “Home”, or the button in “Records” next to the wallets.
  • Transfers work only between wallets of the same currency. A currency exchange cannot be recorded as a transfer yet.
  • Transfers for the period are listed at the bottom of “Records”. Tap a transfer to change or delete it.

If you delete a wallet

The records stay, but without a wallet. Transfers to and from it are deleted.
