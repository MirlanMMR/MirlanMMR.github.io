Categories

A category answers “on what” or “from where”: groceries, phone, salary. Expenses and income have separate lists of categories.

Where they are

“Settings” → “Expense categories” and “Income categories”. Tap a category to open its card, or “+” in the section header to add a new one.

A new category can also be created while entering a record: in the record card choose “New” and type the name.

A ready-made set

“Settings” → “Add recommended categories” shows a list of common categories. Tick the ones you need and tap “Add”. Those you already have are not created twice.

What is in the category card

FieldWhat it does
NameWhat the category is called in lists.
IconPicked from the name automatically until you choose your own. Any emoji works.
ColorThe colour of the slice on the chart and of the icon. The same on every screen.
SubcategoriesAn optional split inside the category.
What kind of expense is thisThe kind of spending for “Statistics”: essentials, hobbies and sport, little joys, unexpected. Assigned from the name automatically; you can change it.
Monthly limitHow much you are ready to spend on the category in a month. See “Limits”.
Remember the amountWhen the category is picked in a new record, the last amount is filled in. Handy for payments of the same amount each time: a phone plan, rent.

Subcategories

A subcategory splits a category into parts: “spouse” and “kids” under “Phone”, “fuel” and “repairs” under “Car”. The category's amount includes all its subcategories.

  • Add one in the category card: “Add your own subcategory”, or the ready options under “You can add”.
  • A subcategory is optional in a record.
  • “Suggest subcategory when entering” sets whether the record card shows the subcategory choice: “Ask”, “Always show”, “Don't suggest”.

Move or merge

In the category card: “Move to another category…”. Choose where.

  • If the chosen category has the same name, or already has a subcategory with that name, they merge: records and subcategories are combined.
  • Otherwise the category becomes a subcategory of the chosen one. All records stay.

Deleting and the trash

“Delete” in the card sends the category or subcategory to the trash for 90 days.

  • The records are not deleted. Records of a deleted subcategory stay in the category without a subcategory; records of a deleted category stay without a category.
  • Restore it in “Settings”, section “Trash”: the category comes back with its records.
  • “Clear” in the trash erases its contents forever.

Records without a category

A record can be saved without a category. On “Home” such records are the grey slice of the chart; in “Records” they are the “No category” line. A category can be set later by opening the record.
