Payments

“Payments” holds what repeats every month on the same day: a loan, an installment, rent, a plan. Expected receipts, an advance and a salary, go here too. The app reminds you ahead of time and takes them into the forecast.

How to set up a payment

The “Payments” tab → “Add payment”.

FieldWhat to write in it
NameMortgage, installment, rent.
AmountThe payment for the month. You can calculate: 12000+500.
Pay on day …The day of the month.
First paymentThe month the schedule starts from.
Remind … days beforeHow many days before the due date the payment shows in reminders. Zero means on the payment day.
How many paymentsThe number of payments, or “Endless”.
CategoryThe category the expense is recorded in. For a receipt, choose an income category.

Below is the “Payment schedule”: dates, paid marks and “Total to pay”.

If no category is chosen, payments are recorded as “No category”.

A payment straight from a record

The expense card has a “Regular payment” tick. Set it, give the payment date and the reminder time, and the payment appears in the “Payments” tab.

  • “Payment already made”: the amount goes to expenses right away.
  • “Payment not made yet”: it goes to expenses when you tap “Pay”.

The payment card

The card shows the amount and the day, how many payments are made and how much remains to pay, and the date of the next payment.

ButtonWhat it does
PayRecords an expense for the payment amount with today's date and marks the payment as paid. For a receipt the button is “Received” and records income.
Already recordedMarks the payment as paid without recording anything. For when you have already entered the expense yourself.
Pay earlyPaying ahead: for several months, with any amount, or “Pay off fully”. Available for payments with a set number.

“Pay” and “Already recorded” become available as many days before the due date as “Remind” says. Until then the card says “Payment opens on …”.

Reminders

“Settings” → “Reminders”. One choice for payments and limits:

OptionWhat happens
In the app onlyAn “Attention” line on “Home”. Nothing arrives from outside.
In the app and a badge dotQuiet, no sound: a dot on the app icon until you open it.
Regular notificationsIn the notification shade, with sound. They arrive even when the app is closed. The phone asks for permission to notify.
Don't remindNo line, no notifications.

Upcoming payments are visible without reminders too: the “Upcoming payments” strip in “Records” and “Statistics”, and the “Still ahead this month” block in “Statistics”.

Edit, reset, delete

  • Edit: the button in the payment card.
  • “Reset payment marks”: removes all marks. Expenses already recorded stay.
  • Delete the payment: the schedule disappears; expenses already recorded stay in “Records”.
